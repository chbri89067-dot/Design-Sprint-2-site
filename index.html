Let the record fall.

Lubudde’s three visual simulations turn reports into falling rats. Scroll through each borough to move the film forward, or use its playback controls.

Each falling rat represents a formal report in the visual study. The films cover the Bronx, Staten Island, and Queens. They illustrate reported sightings, not a measured rat population.

Sources & context

Behind the record.

The dataset covers January 1, 2010 through September 16, 2017. Report counts reflect what people reported, along with differences in reporting activity. They do not measure population size or sightings per square mile.
